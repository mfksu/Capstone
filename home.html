Capstone Project — Diane Loves Living

Diane Loves Living

Diane Loves Living is a caregiving organization that supports individuals with mental and physical disabilities. To foster community and help clients develop essential social and practical skills, the organization hosts various community events. However, the business currently lacks a standardized system to measure event attendance and lacks automated interventions to reduce no-show rates.


The goal of this project is to resolve these operational challenges by implementing a cohesive and low-cost technological solution that can be maintained by non-technical staff and that integrates into the organization's existing ecosystem. Our team focused on creating a system that is financially modest and easy for non-technical employees to maintain.

Eventbrite

A central part of Diane Loves Living mission involves hosting events that build community and provide clients with practical life skills.

Currently, there is no reliable method to track who attends the events. Our proof of concept utilizes Eventbrite to provide attendance metrics via Eventbrite's QR code ticketing system.

Acuity Scheduling

Acuity Scheduling is the backbone of the day-to-day operations of the business. Embedded within the Squarespace website of the business Acuity Scheduling handles the consultation and service booking. Acuity Scheduling widgets allow clients to easily see and select available times from hours that are set by the business owner. Integrating Acuity with Eventbrite, through a Zapier automation, allows for time to be automatically blocked off from being booked by a client, ensuring that there will be no scheduling conflicts.

Wave

Wave is an accounting and payment processing platform used for invoicing and collections, and it is the designated processor for our project sponsor. Because Acuity Scheduling does not natively support Wave, we integrated the two platforms using Zapier. This automation allows appointments booked in Acuity to automatically generate draft invoices in Wave based on the client's selections, while simultaneously populating the 'Customers' section for streamlined record-keeping.
